Framework:

Summary of Vue:
  • Pros:
    • Decent developer experience
    • Richer environment
  • Cons:
    • Overhead from virtual DOM
    • Some boilerplate
Summary of Svelte:
  • Pros:
    • Great developer experience
    • Similar to vanilla HTML/CSS/JS
    • Compiled, so no virtual DOM
  • Cons:
    • Ecosystem is lacking
    • Less industry adoption
We decided to go with Svelte.
